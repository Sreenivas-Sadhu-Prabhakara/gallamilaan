Why UPI is kept out

No billing app does a physical, cash-only till reconciliation. If UPI sales were folded into the count, the drawer would show a fake shortage from day one.

Expected cash = opening + cash sales − expenses − payouts. The gap against your count is your real shrinkage — give it a number.
